Intranet

Intranet
A private network that uses Internet protocol technologies to share information and resources within an organization.
It is typically used for internal communication and collaboration between employees.
Intranets can include various applications and services such as email, file sharing, and databases.
Access to an intranet is usually restricted to authorized users within the organization.
